Send the following to the contact in [Section 7](#7-contact-us) with the subject line “Copyright Report”:

1. Your name, address, email, and phone number.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim was infringed.
3. The exact URL(s) — Studio post links, R2 asset paths, or the Discord message/snowflake — plus the Discord snowflake of the uploading user if you know it.
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the disputed use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.

We aim to disable access to reported material within 3 business days of a complete notice, and we tell the affected user that their content was removed and why.

A user whose content was removed may send a counter-notice containing their contact details, identification of the removed material, a statement under penalty of perjury that it was removed by mistake or misidentification, and consent to jurisdiction. We forward a valid counter-notice to the complainant; if they do not act within 10–14 business days, we may restore the material.
